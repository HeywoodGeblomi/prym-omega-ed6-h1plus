1. **Global Measure:** We do NOT claim the measure-average Lyapunov spectrum is proved. The (4/7, 2/7) values are conjectural.
2. **Random Walk:** We do NOT claim the multi-twist random walk approximates the Rauzy-Veech induction measure (isotropy observed).

4. **Individual exponents:** The global values λ₁⁺ = 4/7 and λ₂⁺ = 2/7 remain a conjecture supported by recurrent exact 2:1 monodromy on closed geodesics of the X(1,1) prototype. They are not theorems of this scaffold.
5. **Sum and H⁻:** The sum 6/7 and the H⁻ = ±1/7 are due exclusively to EKZ / Möller / Eskin–Matheus and are not claimed as results of this work.
