# Non-Claims

1. **Discovery of the spectrum.** This repository does **not** claim discovery of $\lambda_1^+=4/7$ or $\lambda_2^+=2/7$. Those values follow from Yu–Zuo’s Weierstrass filtration on the even component of $\Omega\mathcal{M}_4(6)$ together with Möller’s spin-parity containment $W_D(6)\subset$ even. See [docs/SPECTRUM_ATTRIBUTION.md](docs/SPECTRUM_ATTRIBUTION.md).

2. **Primary proofs.** We do not claim a primary proof of the filtration, of the spin parity, of the EKZ sum $6/7$, or of $H^-=\pm 1/7$.

3. **Computational global claim.** No numerical experiment in this repository is promoted as a determination of the global measure-average exponents. `promote_ready = false` for any such claim.

4. **Path-local artefacts.** Recurrent exact 2:1 anisotropy on closed multi-twist geodesics is scoped as path-local / formal geometric evidence only. It is not a substitute for the literature resolution.

5. **Random walks / integrators.** Discrete multi-twist random walks and any continuous integrator runs included as artefacts are not claimed to sample the Masur–Veech measure.

6. **Existence ceiling.** Emptiness of $W_D(2g-2)$ for $g\ge 5$ is due to Gutiérrez-Romo–Pardo et al., not to this work.
