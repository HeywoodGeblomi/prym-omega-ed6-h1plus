# Non-Claims

This file exists to prevent misreading of the computational note and artefacts.

1. **Global measure-average.**  
   We do **not** claim that the Masur–Veech / EKZ measure-average Lyapunov spectrum on $H_1^+$ for $\Omega E_D(6)$ is proved. The values $(4/7,\ 2/7)$ are a **conjecture** consistent with recurrent exact $2:1$ ratios on closed multi-twist geodesics and with published rough numerics. The sum $6/7$ is due to EKZ alone.

2. **Random walk.**  
   We do **not** claim that a multi-twist random walk approximates the Rauzy–Veech or Teichmüller measure. The observed isotropy ($\approx 0.43/0.43$) is a property of that discrete generating set only.

3. **Continuous integrator.**  
   No continuous Rauzy–Veech / Gauss–Manin integrator results are included in this release. Attempts under elementary monodromy remained parabolic and are archived as negative.

4. **Attribution.**  
   - Sum $\lambda_1^+ + \lambda_2^+ = 6/7$: EKZ  
   - $H^- = \pm 1/7$: Möller / EKZ  
   - Individual exponents: **unknown** in the literature (Eskin–Matheus, arXiv:1210.2157)  
   This repository does not claim those values.

5. **Promotion.**  
   `promote_ready = false` for any global computational claim of the individual exponents.

## What is claimed

Recurrent exact $2:1$ anisotropy on closed Teichmüller geodesics generated by Prym-invariant affine multi-twists on the X(1,1) prototype surface in $\Omega E_5(6)$.
