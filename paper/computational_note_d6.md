# Recurrent Exact 2:1 Anisotropy in the Prym-Invariant Spectrum of $\Omega E_5(6)$

**Computational note (draft)**  
**Status:** experimental geometric evidence; global measure-average not claimed.  
**promote_ready:** false

## Abstract

We exhibit a family of Prym-invariant affine multi-twists on the X(1,1) prototype surface in the Weierstrass Prym locus $\Omega E_5(6)$ whose induced Kontsevich–Zorich monodromy on the 4-dimensional block $H_1^+$ has exact raw Lyapunov spectrum $[1,\ 1/2,\ -1/2,\ -1]$. Along the corresponding closed Teichmüller geodesics the anisotropy ratio is exactly $2:1$. The ratio recurs across at least 20 independent words. A random walk on the same generators is nearly isotropic. Scaling the periodic ratio by the known EKZ sum $6/7$ produces the candidate $(4/7,\ 2/7)$, which lies in the range of the only published rough numerics for this open problem. We conjecture that the Masur–Veech / EKZ measure-average exponents on $H_1^+$ are $4/7$ and $2/7$.

## 2. Geometric setup

Let $X(1,1)$ be the standard X-shaped prototype of discriminant $D=5$ (McMullen / Lanneau–Nguyen / arXiv:2210.13503). The surface lies in $\Omega E_5(6)$ by the prototype construction.

Absolute homology is spanned by four horizontal and four vertical cylinder cores with the standard intersection form. The Prym involution splits $H_1 = H_1^+ \oplus H_1^-$ into two 4-dimensional symplectic subspaces. We work on a geometric basis of $H_1^+$ built from the Prym-invariant combinations of these cores.

Prym-invariant multi-twists extend to affine diffeomorphisms with derivatives in $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{R})$.

## 3. Exact 2:1 monodromy

**Observation.** There exist words in the Prym-invariant multi-twist generators whose induced action $M_+$ on the geometric $H_1^+$ basis has raw Lyapunov spectrum exactly $[1,\ 1/2,\ -1/2,\ -1]$.

**Example.** The word $[(1,3),(2,4),(2,4),(3,2),(0,2)]$ yields

$$M_+ = \begin{pmatrix}-15&0&-2&0\\0&1&0&-3\\8&0&1&0\\0&2&0&-5\end{pmatrix},\qquad A = \begin{pmatrix}31&10\\65&21\end{pmatrix}\in\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{R}).$$

Along the closed Teichmüller geodesic generated by $A$, the $H_1^+$ Lyapunov exponents normalized by $\log\rho(A)$ are exactly $2/3$ and $1/3$ (ratio $2:1$).

## 5. Random-walk contrast

A long random walk on the same generators produces a nearly isotropic spectrum $\lambda_1^+ \approx \lambda_2^+ \approx 0.43$ (after scaling to sum $6/7$). The $2:1$ ratio is a property of special closed orbits, not of the naive average over this generating set.

## 6. Candidate and conjecture

Scaling the exact periodic ratio $2:1$ by the known EKZ sum $6/7$ produces $4/7$ and $2/7$.

**Conjecture.** The Masur–Veech / EKZ measure-average Lyapunov exponents of the Kontsevich–Zorich cocycle on $H_1^+$ for Teichmüller curves in $\Omega E_D(6)$ are $\lambda_1^+ = 4/7$ and $\lambda_2^+ = 2/7$.

## 7. Claim boundary

- Established: recurrent exact $2:1$ ratio on closed Teichmüller geodesics from Prym-invariant affine multi-twists on a prototype surface in $\Omega E_5(6)$.
- Conjectural: the measure-average equals $4/7$ and $2/7$.
- Not claimed: a rigorous determination of the global exponents.
