## Statement

Weierstrass Prym eigenforms \(W_D(2g-2)\) exist only for \(g\le 4\).  
In genus 4 the whole family lies in the even component of \(\Omega\mathcal{M}_4(6)\).  
Yu–Zuo’s Weierstrass filtration on that component forces

\[
\lambda_1^+ = 4/7,\qquad \lambda_2^+ = 2/7
\]

for every admissible discriminant \(D\).

The odd component carries the contrasting rigid spectrum \(3/7,2/7,1/7\).
